font-optical-sizing

Choose font-optical-sizing when the design needs different size-based optical font variations. It changes the presentation or flow of writing.

A rule using font-optical-sizing needs a value after the colon.

A small font-optical-sizing example looks like this:

font-optical-sizing: auto;

How this sample works: In this case font-optical-sizing receives auto.
