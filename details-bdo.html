bdo

When you write bdo, you are marking something as overrides text direction. It works on a small piece of text inside a sentence, like placing a useful label or emphasis around selected words.

The opening bdo tag starts its area and the matching closing tag ends it. Keep the opening tag before the affected words and the closing tag immediately after them.

This short sample shows bdo in use:

<h1>Famous seaside songs</h1>

<p>The English song "Oh I do like to be beside the seaside"</p>

<p>
  Looks like this in Hebrew:
  <span dir="rtl">אה, אני אוהב להיות ליד חוף הים</span>
</p>

<p>
  In the computer's memory, this is stored as
  <bdo dir="ltr">אה, אני אוהב להיות ליד חוף הים</bdo>
</p>

Reading the example: The key piece in this sample is <bdo>. Read outward from that tag to see what belongs to it and how it is configured.
