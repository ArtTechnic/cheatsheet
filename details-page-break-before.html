page-break-before

Think of page-break-before as the control for page break before element. It controls how content is divided across columns, pages, or separate pieces.

Place a colon after page-break-before, then supply the value you want. When testing this property, remember: the browser may need enough content before the effect becomes obvious.

Important: This is an older feature. You may meet it in old websites, but you should normally choose a modern alternative for new work.

Here is a compact page-break-before example you can inspect and alter:

page-break-before: auto;

What this example does: The important instruction in the sample is auto. It is assigned to page-break-before and changes only the part described above.
