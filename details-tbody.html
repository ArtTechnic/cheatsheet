tbody

The tbody element holds the main data rows of your table. If your table is a report card, the header row goes in thead, the footer (like a total) goes in tfoot, and all the actual student grades go in tbody. It is the "meat" of the table.

In a properly structured table, tbody wraps every single data row between thead and tfoot. Writing it yourself (instead of letting the browser add it automatically) makes your code clearer to read and easier to style with CSS. Even though browsers will insert a tbody for you if you forget, it is better practice to type it out yourself.

Always keep your tbody after the thead and before the tfoot in your code. The browser will display them in the correct visual order regardless, but keeping the source order consistent makes your HTML easier to follow.



<table>
    <thead>
        <tr><th>Name</th><th>Score</th></tr>
    </thead>
    <tbody>
        <tr><td>Alex</td><td>8</td></tr>
        <tr><td>Sam</td><td>9</td></tr>
    </tbody>
</table>
