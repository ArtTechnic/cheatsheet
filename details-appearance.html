appearance

The browser looks at appearance when working out removes native styling of form elements. It changes one particular part of the browser’s styling calculation.

Different values give appearance different instructions. Try them one at a time, and remember that change only this property while practising, then compare the before and after result.

This short sample shows appearance in use:

appearance: auto;

The important part: Read the line as ‘set appearance to auto.’ That value is the instruction controlling what you see.
