clip-path

The browser looks at clip-path when working out clipping region. It helps decide whether a box is shown and what happens when its contents do not fit normally.

Different values give clip-path different instructions. Try them one at a time, and remember that hidden or clipped content may become impossible to reach, so test with more content than you expect.

Use this clip-path sample as a starting point, then change one piece at a time:

clip-path: circle(40%);

The important part: The colon separates clip-path from its chosen value, circle(40%). The semicolon marks the end of this declaration.
