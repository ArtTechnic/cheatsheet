source

In an HTML document, source represents media/source for picture/video/audio. This tag helps place visual or audio material on the page, or supplies information that belongs with that material.

Unlike most tags, source is a void element: it has no closing tag and holds no content between tags. Remember that visitors may not see or hear the media, so provide useful text alternatives or captions when the tag supports them.

A practical source example is shown below:

<video controls width="250" height="200" muted>

  <source src="https://mdn.github.iohttps://mdn.github.iohttps://mdn.github.io/shared-assets/videos/flower.mp4" type="video/mp4" />
