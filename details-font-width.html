font-width

Changing font-width changes width property for fonts. It helps decide how large a box or part of a box becomes.

Place a colon after font-width, then supply the value you want. When testing this property, remember: padding and borders can also affect the final visible size, especially when box-sizing changes.

Important: This feature is experimental. Check browser support before relying on it.

Here is a compact font-width example you can inspect and alter:

font-width: condensed;

The important part: The colon separates font-width from its chosen value, condensed. The semicolon marks the end of this declaration.
