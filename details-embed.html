embed

The name embed tells you what it represents: embedded content. It places another resource or browsing area inside the current page, like making a window that looks into something else.

Because embed cannot wrap text or other elements, it appears as one void tag. Embedded material can affect security, speed, and accessibility, so include only resources you trust and actually need.

Important: This is an older feature. You may meet it in old websites, but you should normally choose a modern alternative for new work.

Use this embed sample as a starting point, then change one piece at a time:

<embed
  type="image/jpeg"
  src="https://mdn.github.iohttps://mdn.github.iohttps://mdn.github.io/shared-assets/images/examples/flowers.jpg"
  width="250"
  height="200" />

How this sample works: The key piece in this sample is <embed>. Read outward from that tag to see what belongs to it and how it is configured.
