rotate

In CSS, rotate is the setting for rotation transform of element. It controls movement or a visual change over time or space.

The value written after rotate: chooses the result. While experimenting, keep in mind that use gentle motion and remember that some visitors ask their device to reduce animation.

The code below shows a simple use of rotate:

rotate: none;

How this sample works: The colon separates rotate from its chosen value, none. The semicolon marks the end of this declaration.
