datalist

Use datalist when a piece of HTML represents predefined options for input. It is a form building block: forms let a visitor enter information, choose something, or start an action.

Put the content belonging to datalist between its opening and closing tags. Every control needs a clear label, and the page should still make sense when somebody uses only a keyboard.

Use this datalist sample as a starting point, then change one piece at a time:

<label for="ice-cream-choice">Choose a flavor:</label>
<input list="ice-cream-flavors" id="ice-cream-choice" name="ice-cream-choice" />

<datalist id="ice-cream-flavors">
  <option value="Chocolate"></option>
  <option value="Coconut"></option>
  <option value="Mint"></option>
  <option value="Strawberry"></option>
  <option value="Vanilla"></option>
</datalist>

The important part: Here datalist supplies the meaning described above; the surrounding tags provide the context needed for the example to make sense.
