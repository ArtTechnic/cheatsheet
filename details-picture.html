picture

When you write picture, you are marking something as responsive image container. This tag helps place visual or audio material on the page, or supplies information that belongs with that material.

Treat the two picture tags like the covers of a folder: the related content goes between them. Remember that visitors may not see or hear the media, so provide useful text alternatives or captions when the tag supports them.

This short sample shows picture in use:

<!--Change the browser window width to see the image change.-->

<picture>
  <source
    srcset="https://mdn.github.iohttps://mdn.github.iohttps://mdn.github.io/shared-assets/images/examples/surfer.jpg"
    media="(orientation: portrait)" />
  <img src="https://mdn.github.iohttps://mdn.github.iohttps://mdn.github.io/shared-assets/images/examples/painted-hand.jpg" alt="" />
</picture>

What to notice: The key piece in this sample is <picture>. Read outward from that tag to see what belongs to it and how it is configured.
