b

When you write b, you are marking something as bold text. It works on a small piece of text inside a sentence, like placing a useful label or emphasis around selected words.

Anything meant to belong to b normally sits inside the pair of tags. Keep the opening tag before the affected words and the closing tag immediately after them.

Here is a compact b example you can inspect and alter:

<p>
  The two most popular science courses offered by the school are
  <b class="term">chemistry</b> (the study of chemicals and the composition of
  substances) and <b class="term">physics</b> (the study of the nature and
  properties of matter and energy).
</p>

What this example does: Here b supplies the meaning described above; the surrounding tags provide the context needed for the example to make sense.
