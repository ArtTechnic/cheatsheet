animation-direction

The animation-direction property controls direction. It controls movement or a visual change over time or space.

CSS reads this as the name animation-direction, a colon, and then a value. As you compare values, use gentle motion and remember that some visitors ask their device to reduce animation.

This short sample shows animation-direction in use:

animation-direction: normal;

What to notice: The important instruction in the sample is normal. It is assigned to animation-direction and changes only the part described above.
