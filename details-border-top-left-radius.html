border-top-left-radius

Think of border-top-left-radius as the control for radius of top-left corner. It changes a line, edge, or corner around an element.

Place a colon after border-top-left-radius, then supply the value you want. When testing this property, remember: borders take up space in the box; outlines usually draw outside it without changing the layout.

A practical border-top-left-radius example is shown below:

border-top-left-radius: 80px 80px;

Reading the example: The important instruction in the sample is 80px 80px. It is assigned to border-top-left-radius and changes only the part described above.
