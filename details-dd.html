dd

When you write dd, you are marking something as description in dl. It is one of the building blocks used to organise related items into a list instead of leaving them as loose paragraphs.

Put the content belonging to dd between its opening and closing tags. Lists have a container and items inside it, so check that each item is nested inside the correct kind of list.

Use this dd sample as a starting point, then change one piece at a time:

<p>Cryptids of Cornwall:</p>

<dl>
  <dt>Beast of Bodmin</dt>
  <dd>A large feline inhabiting Bodmin Moor.</dd>

  <dt>Morgawr</dt>
  <dd>A sea serpent.</dd>

  <dt>Owlman</dt>
  <dd>A giant owl-like creature.</dd>
</dl>

The important part: Start with the dd tag in the code, then inspect what sits inside it and which attributes have been added to its opening tag.
