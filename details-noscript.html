noscript

When you write noscript, you are marking something as scripting fallback. This tag supports how a page is built or behaves rather than representing an ordinary paragraph or picture.

Anything meant to belong to noscript normally sits inside the pair of tags. Place it in the correct part of the document and use it only when that extra behaviour or reusable structure is needed.

A practical noscript example is shown below:

<noscript>
  <!-- anchor linking to external file -->
  <a href="https://www.mozilla.org/">External Link</a>
</noscript>
<p>Rocks!</p>

The important part: This sample puts noscript into a small piece of realistic HTML. Change its content or one attribute at a time and watch what differs.
