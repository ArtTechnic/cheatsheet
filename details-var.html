var

Use var when a piece of HTML represents variable. It works on a small piece of text inside a sentence, like placing a useful label or emphasis around selected words.

Anything meant to belong to var normally sits inside the pair of tags. Keep the opening tag before the affected words and the closing tag immediately after them.

This short sample shows var in use:

<p>
  The volume of a box is <var>l</var> × <var>w</var> × <var>h</var>, where
  <var>l</var> represents the length, <var>w</var> the width and
  <var>h</var> the height of the box.
</p>

What this example does: The key piece in this sample is <var>. Read outward from that tag to see what belongs to it and how it is configured.
