time

In an HTML document, time represents date or time value. It works on a small piece of text inside a sentence, like placing a useful label or emphasis around selected words.

The opening time tag starts its area and the matching closing tag ends it. Keep the opening tag before the affected words and the closing tag immediately after them.

A small time example looks like this:

<p>
  The Cure will be celebrating their 40th anniversary on
  <time datetime="2018-07-07">July 7</time> in London's Hyde Park.
</p>

<p>
  The concert starts at <time datetime="20:00">20:00</time> and you'll be able
  to enjoy the band for at least <time datetime="PT2H30M">2h 30m</time>.
</p>

What this example does: Start with the time tag in the code, then inspect what sits inside it and which attributes have been added to its opening tag.
