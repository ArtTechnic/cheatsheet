figcaption

Browsers read figcaption as a label meaning figure caption. This tag helps place visual or audio material on the page, or supplies information that belongs with that material.

The opening figcaption tag starts its area and the matching closing tag ends it. Remember that visitors may not see or hear the media, so provide useful text alternatives or captions when the tag supports them.

Try the following figcaption code in a spare practice file:

<figure>
  <img
    src="https://mdn.github.iohttps://mdn.github.iohttps://mdn.github.io/shared-assets/images/examples/elephant.jpg"
    alt="Elephant at sunset" />
  <figcaption>An elephant at sunset</figcaption>
</figure>

What to notice: Start with the figcaption tag in the code, then inspect what sits inside it and which attributes have been added to its opening tag.
