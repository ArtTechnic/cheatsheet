break-inside

The browser looks at break-inside when working out avoid page/column breaks inside. It controls how content is divided across columns, pages, or separate pieces.

Add break-inside inside a CSS rule and follow it with a colon and value. A helpful beginner check is this: The browser may need enough content before the effect becomes obvious.

Here is a break-inside example to try in a practice page:

break-inside: auto;

What this example does: Here, auto is the value attached to break-inside. Change it, refresh the page, and compare the new result with the original.
