li

Use li when a piece of HTML represents list item. It is one of the building blocks used to organise related items into a list instead of leaving them as loose paragraphs.

Put the content belonging to li between its opening and closing tags. Lists have a container and items inside it, so check that each item is nested inside the correct kind of list.

Important: This is an older feature. You may meet it in old websites, but you should normally choose a modern alternative for new work.

A small li example looks like this:

<p>Apollo astronauts:</p>

<ul>
  <li>Neil Armstrong</li>
  <li>Alan Bean</li>
  <li>Peter Conrad</li>
  <li>Edgar Mitchell</li>
  <li>Alan Shepard</li>
</ul>

The important part: The key piece in this sample is <li>. Read outward from that tag to see what belongs to it and how it is configured.
