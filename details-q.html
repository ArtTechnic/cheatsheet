q

The name q tells you what it represents: inline quotation. This tag helps organise writing so readers and browsers can understand how the words fit together.

The opening q tag starts its area and the matching closing tag ends it. Do not choose it just because of its default size or spacing; choose it when its meaning matches the writing.

A small q example looks like this:

<p>
  When Dave asks HAL to open the pod bay door, HAL answers:
  <q
    cite="https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0062622/quotes/?item=qt0396921&ref_=ext_shr_lnk">
    I'm sorry, Dave. I'm afraid I can't do that.
  </q>
</p>

What this example does: Here q supplies the meaning described above; the surrounding tags provide the context needed for the example to make sense.
