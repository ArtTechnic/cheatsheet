padding-right

In CSS, padding-right is the setting for right padding. It changes space around, inside, or between boxes.

Different values give padding-right different instructions.

Use this padding-right sample as a starting point, then change one piece at a time:

padding-right: 1.5em;

How this sample works: The colon separates padding-right from its chosen value, 1.5em.
