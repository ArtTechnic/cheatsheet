p

The p tag marks part of the page as paragraph. This tag helps organise writing so readers and browsers can understand how the words fit together.

Treat the two p tags like the covers of a folder: the related content goes between them. Do not choose it just because of its default size or spacing; choose it when its meaning matches the writing.

Try the following p code in a spare practice file:

<p>
  Geckos are a group of usually small, usually nocturnal lizards. They are found
  on every continent except Antarctica.
</p>

<p>
  Some species live in houses where they hunt insects attracted by artificial
  light.
</p>

Reading the example: Start with the p tag in the code, then inspect what sits inside it and which attributes have been added to its opening tag.
