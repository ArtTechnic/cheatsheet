text-decoration

Use text-decoration when you need to change decoration for text. It changes the presentation or flow of writing.

Place a colon after text-decoration, then supply the value you want.

Here is a text-decoration example to try in a practice page:

text-decoration: underline;

The important part: In this case text-decoration receives underline.
