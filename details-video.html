video

Browsers read video as a label meaning video media. This tag helps place visual or audio material on the page, or supplies information that belongs with that material.

Put the content belonging to video between its opening and closing tags. Remember that visitors may not see or hear the media, so provide useful text alternatives or captions when the tag supports them.

Important: This feature is experimental. Check browser support before relying on it.

A practical video example is shown below:
