blockquote

When you write blockquote, you are marking something as quoted section. This tag helps organise writing so readers and browsers can understand how the words fit together.

Put the content belonging to blockquote between its opening and closing tags. Do not choose it just because of its default size or spacing; choose it when its meaning matches the writing.

The code below shows a simple use of blockquote:

<div>
  <blockquote cite="https://www.huxley.net/bnw/four.html">
    <p>
      Words can be like X-rays, if you use them properly—they'll go through
      anything. You read and you're pierced.
    </p>
  </blockquote>
  <p>—Aldous Huxley, <cite>Brave New World</cite></p>
</div>

How this sample works: Start with the blockquote tag in the code, then inspect what sits inside it and which attributes have been added to its opening tag.
