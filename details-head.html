head

The name head tells you what it represents: document metadata container. Think of a web page as a labelled storage box: this tag marks one of the large, important parts of that box.

Anything meant to belong to head normally sits inside the pair of tags. Use it for its intended page section rather than merely to change the appearance; CSS handles the appearance.

Important: This is an older feature. You may meet it in old websites, but you should normally choose a modern alternative for new work.

Here is a compact head example you can inspect and alter:

<!doctype html>
<html lang="en-US">
  <head>
    <meta charset="UTF-8" />
    <meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width" />
    <title>Document title</title>
  </head>
</html>

What to notice: This sample puts head into a small piece of realistic HTML. Change its content or one attribute at a time and watch what differs.
