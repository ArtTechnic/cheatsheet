overflow

Changing overflow changes overflow behavior. It helps decide whether a box is shown and what happens when its contents do not fit normally.

The value written after overflow: chooses the result. While experimenting, keep in mind that hidden or clipped content may become impossible to reach, so test with more content than you expect.

Here is a compact overflow example you can inspect and alter:

overflow: visible;

How this sample works: The sample asks the browser to use visible for overflow. The property stays the same when you experiment; the value is the part to change.
