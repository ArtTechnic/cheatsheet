text-decoration-thickness

The text-decoration-thickness property controls thickness of text decoration. It changes the presentation or flow of writing.

Add text-decoration-thickness inside a CSS rule and follow it with a colon and value.

Here is a compact text-decoration-thickness example you can inspect and alter:

text-decoration-line: underline;
text-decoration-thickness: 3px;

The important part: The colon separates text-decoration-thickness from its chosen value, underline.
