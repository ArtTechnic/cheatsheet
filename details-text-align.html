text-align

Think of text-align as the control for inline content alignment. It helps arrange children inside a layout.

CSS reads this as the name text-align, a colon, and then a value.

Important: This is not a standard feature, so different browsers may handle it differently. Treat it as something to recognize rather than a dependable first choice.

Use this text-align sample as a starting point, then change one piece at a time:

text-align: start;

What to notice: In this case text-align receives start.
