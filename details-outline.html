outline

The browser looks at outline when working out outline outside border. It changes a line, edge, or corner around an element.

CSS reads this as the name outline, a colon, and then a value. As you compare values, borders take up space in the box; outlines usually draw outside it without changing the layout.

Here is a compact outline example you can inspect and alter:

outline: solid;

How this sample works: The colon separates outline from its chosen value, solid. The semicolon marks the end of this declaration.
