wbr

When you write wbr, you are marking something as word break opportunity. This tag helps organise writing so readers and browsers can understand how the words fit together.

Do not add a separate closing tag for wbr. It is a void element, with its information supplied through attributes. Do not choose it just because of its default size or spacing; choose it when its meaning matches the writing.

A small wbr example looks like this:

<div id="example-paragraphs">
  <p>Fernstraßenbauprivatfinanzierungsgesetz</p>
  <p>Fernstraßen<wbr />bau<wbr />privat<wbr />finanzierungs<wbr />gesetz</p>
  <p>Fernstraßen&shy;bau&shy;privat&shy;finanzierungs&shy;gesetz</p>
</div>

What to notice: Find <wbr> in the sample first. Its attributes and enclosed content provide the information this element needs.
