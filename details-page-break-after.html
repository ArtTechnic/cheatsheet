page-break-after

The page-break-after property controls page break after element. It controls how content is divided across columns, pages, or separate pieces.

Place a colon after page-break-after, then supply the value you want. When testing this property, remember: the browser may need enough content before the effect becomes obvious.

Important: This is an older feature. You may meet it in old websites, but you should normally choose a modern alternative for new work.

A practical page-break-after example is shown below:

page-break-after: auto;

The important part: The important instruction in the sample is auto. It is assigned to page-break-after and changes only the part described above.
