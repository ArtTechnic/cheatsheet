min-width

Choose min-width when the design needs different min width. It helps decide how large a box or part of a box becomes.

The value written after min-width: chooses the result. While experimenting, keep in mind that padding and borders can also affect the final visible size, especially when box-sizing changes.

Use this min-width sample as a starting point, then change one piece at a time:

min-width: 150px;

What to notice: The sample asks the browser to use 150px for min-width. The property stays the same when you experiment; the value is the part to change.
