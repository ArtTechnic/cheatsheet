i

Use i when a piece of HTML represents italic text. It works on a small piece of text inside a sentence, like placing a useful label or emphasis around selected words.

Treat the two i tags like the covers of a folder: the related content goes between them. Keep the opening tag before the affected words and the closing tag immediately after them.

The code below shows a simple use of i:

<p>I looked at it and thought <i>This can't be real!</i></p>

<p>
  <i>Musa</i> is one of two or three genera in the family <i>Musaceae</i>; it
  includes bananas and plantains.
</p>

<p>
  The term <i>bandwidth</i> describes the measure of how much information can
  pass through a data connection in a given amount of time.
</p>

Reading the example: The key piece in this sample is <i>. Read outward from that tag to see what belongs to it and how it is configured.
