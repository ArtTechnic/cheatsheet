hgroup

Browsers read hgroup as a label meaning heading group. Think of it as an HTML building block specifically meant for heading group.

Put the content belonging to hgroup between its opening and closing tags. Use it when that description matches the content, not simply because its default appearance seems useful.

The code below shows a simple use of hgroup:

<hgroup>
  <h1>Frankenstein</h1>
  <p>Or: The Modern Prometheus</p>
</hgroup>
<p>
  Victor Frankenstein, a Swiss scientist, has a great ambition: to create
  intelligent life. But when his creature first stirs, he realizes he has made a
  monster. A monster which, abandoned by his master and shunned by everyone who
  sees it, follows Dr Frankenstein to the very ends of the earth.
</p>

What this example does: The sample demonstrates <hgroup>. Its attributes and enclosed content provide the information this element needs.
