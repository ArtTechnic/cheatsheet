box-shadow

Use box-shadow when you need to change shadow effect. It changes how something looks rather than changing what the HTML means.

Place a colon after box-shadow, then supply the value you want. When testing this property, remember: check that text remains easy to read and that important information does not depend on colour alone.

Here is a compact box-shadow example you can inspect and alter:

box-shadow: 10px 5px 5px red;

Reading the example: Here, 10px 5px 5px red is the value attached to box-shadow. Change it, refresh the page, and compare the new result with the original.
