image-orientation

Choose image-orientation when the design needs different orientation of images. It changes one particular part of the browser’s styling calculation.

Different values give image-orientation different instructions. Try them one at a time, and remember that change only this property while practising, then compare the before and after result.

Here is a compact image-orientation example you can inspect and alter:

image-orientation: none;

Reading the example: The sample asks the browser to use none for image-orientation. The property stays the same when you experiment; the value is the part to change.
