rule-visibility-items

The browser looks at rule-visibility-items when working out defines the rule-visibility-items property. It helps decide whether a box is shown and what happens when its contents do not fit normally.

The value written after rule-visibility-items: chooses the result. While experimenting, keep in mind that hidden or clipped content may become impossible to reach, so test with more content than you expect.

Important: This feature is experimental. Check browser support before relying on it.

This short sample shows rule-visibility-items in use:

rule-visibility-items: all;

The important part: This declaration gives rule-visibility-items the value all. Replace that value with another valid choice to compare the two results.
