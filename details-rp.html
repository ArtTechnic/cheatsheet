rp

The name rp tells you what it represents: ruby parentheses. It works on a small piece of text inside a sentence, like placing a useful label or emphasis around selected words.

The opening rp tag starts its area and the matching closing tag ends it. Keep the opening tag before the affected words and the closing tag immediately after them.

This short sample shows rp in use:

<ruby>
  漢 <rp>(</rp><rt>kan</rt><rp>)</rp> 字 <rp>(</rp><rt>ji</rt><rp>)</rp>
</ruby>

What to notice: The key piece in this sample is <rp>. Read outward from that tag to see what belongs to it and how it is configured.
