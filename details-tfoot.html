tfoot

A tfoot element groups together the footer rows of your table. Footer rows are things like a "Total" line, a "Summary" row, or any final piece of information that applies to the whole table. It sits at the very bottom of the table.

Here is a quirk: in your HTML code, you should write tfoot BEFORE tbody, even though it appears at the bottom of the table visually. This can feel backwards, but browsers handle the display order automatically. You do this so that your code reads top-to-bottom logically: header first, then footer, then body. Some older HTML style guides require it this way too.

You can use tfoot even if your table does not have a tbody. It works on its own for simple cases, like a table that just has one summary row at the bottom.



<table>
    <tbody>
        <tr><td>Pen</td><td>$2</td></tr>
        <tr><td>Pencil</td><td>$1</td></tr>
    </tbody>
    <tfoot>
        <tr><th>Total</th><td>$3</td></tr>
    </tfoot>
</table>

Pencil$1
Total$3
