caret

With caret, you can control shorthand for caret color and shape. It changes part of the way a visitor interacts with an element.

Place a colon after caret, then supply the value you want.

Important: This feature is experimental. Check browser support before relying on it.

Here is a compact caret example you can inspect and alter:

caret: red;

Reading the example: The important instruction in the sample is red. It is assigned to caret and changes only the part described above.
