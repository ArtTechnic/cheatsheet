address

Use address when a piece of HTML represents contact information. It identifies what kind of section this is, rather like putting a label on a folder before placing papers inside it.

Put the content belonging to address between its opening and closing tags. Choose it because the content has that meaning. If you only need an unlabelled box for styling, a div may be the clearer choice.

A practical address example is shown below:

<p>Contact the author of this page:</p>

<address>
  <a href="mailto:jim@example.com">jim@example.com</a><br />
  <a href="tel:+14155550132">+1 (415) 555‑0132</a>
</address>

How this sample works: This sample puts address into a small piece of realistic HTML. Change its content or one attribute at a time and watch what differs.
