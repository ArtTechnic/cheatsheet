border-color

The border-color property controls border color. It changes how something looks rather than changing what the HTML means.

Different values give border-color different instructions. Try them one at a time, and remember that check that text remains easy to read and that important information does not depend on colour alone.

Here is a compact border-color example you can inspect and alter:

border-color: red;

What to notice: This declaration gives border-color the value red. Replace that value with another valid choice to compare the two results.
