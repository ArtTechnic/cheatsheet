transform-origin

Choose transform-origin when the design needs different origin for transform. It controls movement or a visual change over time or space.

Add transform-origin inside a CSS rule and follow it with a colon and value. A helpful beginner check is this: Use gentle motion and remember that some visitors ask their device to reduce animation.

A practical transform-origin example is shown below:

transform-origin: center;

The important part: The sample asks the browser to use center for transform-origin. The property stays the same when you experiment; the value is the part to change.
