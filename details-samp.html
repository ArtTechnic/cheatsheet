samp

The name samp tells you what it represents: sample output. It works on a small piece of text inside a sentence, like placing a useful label or emphasis around selected words.

Treat the two samp tags like the covers of a folder: the related content goes between them. Keep the opening tag before the affected words and the closing tag immediately after them.

This short sample shows samp in use:

<p>I was trying to boot my computer, but I got this hilarious message:</p>

<p>
  <samp>Keyboard not found <br />Press F1 to continue</samp>
</p>

The important part: Here samp supplies the meaning described above; the surrounding tags provide the context needed for the example to make sense.
