option

The name option tells you what it represents: option in select. It is a form building block: forms let a visitor enter information, choose something, or start an action.

Treat the two option tags like the covers of a folder: the related content goes between them. Every control needs a clear label, and the page should still make sense when somebody uses only a keyboard.

Here is a compact option example you can inspect and alter:

<label for="pet-select">Choose a pet:</label>

<select id="pet-select">
  <option value="">--Please choose an option--</option>
  <option value="dog">Dog</option>
  <option value="cat">Cat</option>
  <option value="hamster">Hamster</option>
  <option value="parrot">Parrot</option>
  <option value="spider">Spider</option>
  <option value="goldfish">Goldfish</option>
</select>

What this example does: This sample puts option into a small piece of realistic HTML. Change its content or one attribute at a time and watch what differs.
