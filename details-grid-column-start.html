grid-column-start

Choose grid-column-start when the design needs different start edge of grid item placement. It helps arrange children inside a layout.

Place a colon after grid-column-start, then supply the value you want.

Here is a compact grid-column-start example you can inspect and alter:

grid-column-start: auto;

Reading the example: Here, auto is the value attached to grid-column-start.
