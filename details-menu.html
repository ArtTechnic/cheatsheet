menu

When you write menu, you are marking something as context/menu list. It is one of the building blocks used to organise related items into a list instead of leaving them as loose paragraphs.

Put the content belonging to menu between its opening and closing tags. Lists have a container and items inside it, so check that each item is nested inside the correct kind of list.

Important: This is an older feature. You may meet it in old websites, but you should normally choose a modern alternative for new work.

Here is a menu example to try in a practice page:

<div class="news">
  <a href="#">NASA's Webb Delivers Deepest Infrared Image of Universe Yet</a>
  <menu>
    <li><button id="save">Save for later</button></li>
    <li><button id="share">Share this news</button></li>
  </menu>
</div>

The important part: The sample demonstrates <menu>.
