overflow-x

The overflow-x property controls horizontal overflow. It helps decide whether a box is shown and what happens when its contents do not fit normally.

Place a colon after overflow-x, then supply the value you want. When testing this property, remember: hidden or clipped content may become impossible to reach, so test with more content than you expect.

Here is an overflow-x example to try in a practice page:

overflow-x: visible;

What to notice: The important instruction in the sample is visible. It is assigned to overflow-x and changes only the part described above.
