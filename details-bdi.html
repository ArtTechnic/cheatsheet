bdi

When you write bdi, you are marking something as isolated bidirectional text. It works on a small piece of text inside a sentence, like placing a useful label or emphasis around selected words.

Put the content belonging to bdi between its opening and closing tags. Keep the opening tag before the affected words and the closing tag immediately after them.

A small bdi example looks like this:

<h1>World wrestling championships</h1>

<ul>
  <li><bdi class="name">Evil Steven</bdi>: 1st place</li>
  <li><bdi class="name">François fatale</bdi>: 2nd place</li>
  <li><span class="name">سما</span>: 3rd place</li>
  <li><bdi class="name">الرجل القوي إيان</bdi>: 4th place</li>
  <li><span class="name" dir="auto">سما</span>: 5th place</li>
</ul>

Reading the example: The sample demonstrates <bdi>. Its attributes and enclosed content provide the information this element needs.
