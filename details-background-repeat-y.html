background-repeat-y

Think of background-repeat-y as the control for vertical tiling of background image. It changes how something looks rather than changing what the HTML means.

Add background-repeat-y inside a CSS rule and follow it with a colon and value.

Important: This feature is experimental. Check browser support before relying on it.

This short sample shows background-repeat-y in use:

background-repeat-y: repeat;

What to notice: In this case background-repeat-y receives repeat.
