main

The main tag marks part of the page as main content area. Think of a web page as a labelled storage box: this tag marks one of the large, important parts of that box.

Put the content belonging to main between its opening and closing tags. Use it for its intended page section rather than merely to change the appearance; CSS handles the appearance.

Here is a main example to try in a practice page:

<header>Gecko facts</header>

<main>
  <p>
    Geckos are a group of usually small, usually nocturnal lizards. They are
    found on every continent except Antarctica.
  </p>

  <p>
    Many species of gecko have adhesive toe pads which enable them to climb
    walls and even windows.
  </p>
</main>

The important part: Here main supplies the meaning described above; the surrounding tags provide the context needed for the example to make sense.
