animation-fill-mode

In CSS, animation-fill-mode is the setting for fill mode. It controls movement or a visual change over time or space.

Place a colon after animation-fill-mode, then supply the value you want. When testing this property, remember: use gentle motion and remember that some visitors ask their device to reduce animation.

A small animation-fill-mode example looks like this:

animation-fill-mode: none;
animation-delay: 1s;

The important part: In this case animation-fill-mode receives none. Trying a contrasting valid value makes the difference easier to spot.
