animation-fill-mode

In CSS, animation-fill-mode is the setting for fill mode. It controls movement or a visual change over time or space.

Place a colon after animation-fill-mode, then supply the value you want.

A small animation-fill-mode example looks like this:

animation-fill-mode: none;
animation-delay: 1s;

The important part: In this case animation-fill-mode receives none.
