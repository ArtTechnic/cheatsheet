translate

The translate property controls translation transform of element. It controls movement or a visual change over time or space.

The value written after translate: chooses the result. While experimenting, keep in mind that use gentle motion and remember that some visitors ask their device to reduce animation.

A small translate example looks like this:

translate: none;

What to notice: This declaration gives translate the value none. Replace that value with another valid choice to compare the two results.
