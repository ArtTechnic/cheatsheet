border-inline

Think of border-inline as the control for shorthand for inline-start/end borders. It changes a line, edge, or corner around an element.

Different values give border-inline different instructions. Try them one at a time, and remember that borders take up space in the box; outlines usually draw outside it without changing the layout.

A small border-inline example looks like this:

border-inline: solid;
writing-mode: horizontal-tb;

What to notice: This declaration gives border-inline the value solid. Replace that value with another valid choice to compare the two results.
