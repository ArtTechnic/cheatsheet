em

Use em when a piece of HTML represents emphasized text. It works on a small piece of text inside a sentence, like placing a useful label or emphasis around selected words.

Treat the two em tags like the covers of a folder: the related content goes between them. Keep the opening tag before the affected words and the closing tag immediately after them.

Try the following em code in a spare practice file:

<p>Get out of bed <em>now</em>!</p>

<p>We <em>had</em> to do something about it.</p>

<p>This is <em>not</em> a drill!</p>

How this sample works: The sample demonstrates <em>. Its attributes and enclosed content provide the information this element needs.
