u

The name u tells you what it represents: underlined text. It works on a small piece of text inside a sentence, like placing a useful label or emphasis around selected words.

Treat the two u tags like the covers of a folder: the related content goes between them. Keep the opening tag before the affected words and the closing tag immediately after them.

Important: This is an older feature. You may meet it in old websites, but you should normally choose a modern alternative for new work.

Try the following u code in a spare practice file:

<p>
  You could use this element to highlight <u>speling</u> mistakes, so the writer
  can <u>corect</u> them.
</p>

Reading the example: The key piece in this sample is <u>. Read outward from that tag to see what belongs to it and how it is configured.
