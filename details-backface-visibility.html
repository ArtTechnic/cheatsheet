backface-visibility

Think of backface-visibility as the control for 3D backface visibility. It helps decide whether a box is shown and what happens when its contents do not fit normally.

A rule using backface-visibility needs a value after the colon.

A practical backface-visibility example is shown below:

backface-visibility: visible;

How this sample works: The important instruction in the sample is visible. It is assigned to backface-visibility and changes only the part described above.
