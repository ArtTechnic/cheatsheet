noembed

When you write noembed, you are marking something as embedded-content fallback. Think of it as an HTML building block specifically meant for embedded-content fallback.

Treat the two noembed tags like the covers of a folder: the related content goes between them. Use it when that description matches the content, not simply because its default appearance seems useful.

Important: This is an older feature. You may meet it in old websites, but you should normally choose a modern alternative for new work.

Here is a compact noembed example you can inspect and alter:

<embed
  type="video/webm"
  src="/media/examples/flower.mp4"
  width="200"
  height="200" />
<noembed>
  <h1>Alternative content</h1>
</noembed>

The important part: The key piece in this sample is <noembed>. Read outward from that tag to see what belongs to it and how it is configured.
