font-family

Think of font-family as the control for font family. It changes the presentation or flow of writing.

The value written after font-family: chooses the result. While experimenting, keep in mind that test several lines, long words, and zoomed text—a single short sentence can hide problems.

Here is a compact font-family example you can inspect and alter:

font-family: "Georgia", serif;

The important part: Here, "Georgia", serif is the value attached to font-family. Change it, refresh the page, and compare the new result with the original.
