kbd

The name kbd tells you what it represents: keyboard input. It works on a small piece of text inside a sentence, like placing a useful label or emphasis around selected words.

The opening kbd tag starts its area and the matching closing tag ends it. Keep the opening tag before the affected words and the closing tag immediately after them.

A small kbd example looks like this:

<p>
  Please press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>R</kbd> to re-render an
  MDN page.
</p>

The important part: Here kbd supplies the meaning described above; the surrounding tags provide the context needed for the example to make sense.
