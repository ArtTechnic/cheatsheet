animation-duration

With animation-duration, you can control duration. It controls movement or a visual change over time or space.

Place a colon after animation-duration, then supply the value you want. When testing this property, remember: use gentle motion and remember that some visitors ask their device to reduce animation.

A small animation-duration example looks like this:

animation-duration: 750ms;

Reading the example: The sample asks the browser to use 750ms for animation-duration. The property stays the same when you experiment; the value is the part to change.
