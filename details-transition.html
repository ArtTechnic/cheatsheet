transition

The transition property controls transition shorthand. It controls movement or a visual change over time or space.

Different values give transition different instructions.

Try the following transition code in a spare practice file:

transition: margin-right 2s;

How this sample works: The colon separates transition from its chosen value, margin-right 2s.
