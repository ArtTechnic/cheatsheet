mark

When you write mark, you are marking something as highlighted text. It works on a small piece of text inside a sentence, like placing a useful label or emphasis around selected words.

Anything meant to belong to mark normally sits inside the pair of tags. Keep the opening tag before the affected words and the closing tag immediately after them.

This short sample shows mark in use:

<p>Search results for "salamander":</p>

<hr />

<p>
  Several species of <mark>salamander</mark> inhabit the temperate rainforest of
  the Pacific Northwest.
</p>

<p>
  Most <mark>salamander</mark>s are nocturnal, and hunt for insects, worms, and
  other small creatures.
</p>
