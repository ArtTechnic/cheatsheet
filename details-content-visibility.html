content-visibility

The content-visibility property controls visibility of content for rendering. It helps decide whether a box is shown and what happens when its contents do not fit normally.

Different values give content-visibility different instructions.

The code below shows a simple use of content-visibility:

content-visibility: visible;

Reading the example: This declaration gives content-visibility the value visible.
