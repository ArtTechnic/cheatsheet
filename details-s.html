s

When you write s, you are marking something as no-longer-accurate text. It works on a small piece of text inside a sentence, like placing a useful label or emphasis around selected words.

The opening s tag starts its area and the matching closing tag ends it. Keep the opening tag before the affected words and the closing tag immediately after them.

A practical s example is shown below:

<p><s>There will be a few tickets available at the box office tonight.</s></p>

<p>SOLD OUT!</p>

The important part: The key piece in this sample is <s>. Read outward from that tag to see what belongs to it and how it is configured.
