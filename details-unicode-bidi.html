unicode-bidi

The unicode-bidi property controls bi-directional text handling.

Place a colon after unicode-bidi, then supply the value you want.

A small unicode-bidi example looks like this:

unicode-bidi: normal;

How this sample works: In this case unicode-bidi receives normal.
