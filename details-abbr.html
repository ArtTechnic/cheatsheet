abbr

The name abbr tells you what it represents: abbreviation or acronym. It works on a small piece of text inside a sentence, like placing a useful label or emphasis around selected words.

Anything meant to belong to abbr normally sits inside the pair of tags. Keep the opening tag before the affected words and the closing tag immediately after them.

The code below shows a simple use of abbr:

<p>
  You can use <abbr>CSS</abbr> (Cascading Style Sheets) to style your
  <abbr>HTML</abbr> (HyperText Markup Language). Using style sheets, you can
  keep your <abbr>CSS</abbr> presentation layer and <abbr>HTML</abbr> content
  layer separate. This is called "separation of concerns."
</p>

The important part: Start with the abbr tag in the code, then inspect what sits inside it and which attributes have been added to its opening tag.
