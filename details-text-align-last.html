text-align-last

Use text-align-last when you need to change alignment of last line in block. It helps arrange children inside a layout.

Place a colon after text-align-last, then supply the value you want.

This short sample shows text-align-last in use:

text-align-last: right;

How this sample works: The important instruction in the sample is right. It is assigned to text-align-last and changes only the part described above.
