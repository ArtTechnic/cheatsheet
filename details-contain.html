contain

Choose contain when the design needs different optimization hints for element. It helps decide whether a box is shown and what happens when its contents do not fit normally.

Different values give contain different instructions. Try them one at a time, and remember that hidden or clipped content may become impossible to reach, so test with more content than you expect.

A small contain example looks like this:

contain: none;

Reading the example: This declaration gives contain the value none. Replace that value with another valid choice to compare the two results.
