break-before

Think of break-before as the control for how page/column break before element. It controls how content is divided across columns, pages, or separate pieces.

The value written after break-before: chooses the result. While experimenting, keep in mind that the browser may need enough content before the effect becomes obvious.

A small break-before example looks like this:

break-before: auto;

How this sample works: Read the line as ‘set break-before to auto.’ That value is the instruction controlling what you see.
