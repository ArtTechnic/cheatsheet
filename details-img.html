img

The name img tells you what it represents: image. This tag helps place visual or audio material on the page, or supplies information that belongs with that material.

Do not add a separate closing tag for img. It is a void element, with its information supplied through attributes. Remember that visitors may not see or hear the media, so provide useful text alternatives or captions when the tag supports them.

This short sample shows img in use:

<img
  class="fit-picture"
  src="https://mdn.github.iohttps://mdn.github.iohttps://mdn.github.io/shared-assets/images/examples/grapefruit-slice.jpg"
  alt="Grapefruit slice atop a pile of other slices" />

Reading the example: Here img supplies the meaning described above; the surrounding tags provide the context needed for the example to make sense.
