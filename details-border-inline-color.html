border-inline-color

The border-inline-color property controls color of inline-start/end borders. It changes how something looks rather than changing what the HTML means.

The value written after border-inline-color: chooses the result. While experimenting, keep in mind that check that text remains easy to read and that important information does not depend on colour alone.

Use this border-inline-color sample as a starting point, then change one piece at a time:

border-inline-color: red;
writing-mode: horizontal-tb;

What this example does: The colon separates border-inline-color from its chosen value, red. The semicolon marks the end of this declaration.
