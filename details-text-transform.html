text-transform

Changing text-transform changes case transform. It controls movement or a visual change over time or space.

The value written after text-transform: chooses the result.

Here is a compact text-transform example you can inspect and alter:

text-transform: capitalize;

What to notice: The colon separates text-transform from its chosen value, capitalize.
