area

Browsers read area as a label meaning hotspot in image map. This tag helps place visual or audio material on the page, or supplies information that belongs with that material.

Do not add a separate closing tag for area. It is a void element, with its information supplied through attributes. Remember that visitors may not see or hear the media, so provide useful text alternatives or captions when the tag supports them.

Important: This feature is experimental. Check browser support before relying on it.

Use this area sample as a starting point, then change one piece at a time:
