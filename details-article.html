article

The name article tells you what it represents: self-contained composition. It identifies what kind of section this is, rather like putting a label on a folder before placing papers inside it.

Put the content belonging to article between its opening and closing tags. Choose it because the content has that meaning. If you only need an unlabelled box for styling, a div may be the clearer choice.

This short sample shows article in use:

<article class="forecast">
  <h1>Weather forecast for Seattle</h1>
  <article class="day-forecast">
    <h2>03 March 2018</h2>
    <p>Rain.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="day-forecast">
    <h2>04 March 2018</h2>
    <p>Periods of rain.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="day-forecast">
    <h2>05 March 2018</h2>
    <p>Heavy rain.</p>
  </article>
</article>

How this sample works: This sample puts article into a small piece of realistic HTML. Change its content or one attribute at a time and watch what differs.
