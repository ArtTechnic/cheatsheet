scroll-behavior

With scroll-behavior, you can control smooth scrolling. It helps decide whether a box is shown and what happens when its contents do not fit normally.

Add scroll-behavior inside a CSS rule and follow it with a colon and value.

Here is a compact scroll-behavior example you can inspect and alter:

scroll-behavior: auto;

The important part: This declaration gives scroll-behavior the value auto.
