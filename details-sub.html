sub

Use sub when a piece of HTML represents subscript. It works on a small piece of text inside a sentence, like placing a useful label or emphasis around selected words.

The opening sub tag starts its area and the matching closing tag ends it. Keep the opening tag before the affected words and the closing tag immediately after them.

Try the following sub code in a spare practice file:

<p>
  Almost every developer's favorite molecule is
  C<sub>8</sub>H<sub>10</sub>N<sub>4</sub>O<sub>2</sub>, also known as
  "caffeine."
</p>

How this sample works: The key piece in this sample is <sub>. Read outward from that tag to see what belongs to it and how it is configured.
