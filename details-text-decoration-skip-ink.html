text-decoration-skip-ink

With text-decoration-skip-ink, you can control skips ink of text decoration. It changes the presentation or flow of writing.

A rule using text-decoration-skip-ink needs a value after the colon.

A small text-decoration-skip-ink example looks like this:

text-decoration-skip-ink: auto;

The important part: In this case text-decoration-skip-ink receives auto.
