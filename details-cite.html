cite

The cite tag marks part of the page as citation. It works on a small piece of text inside a sentence, like placing a useful label or emphasis around selected words.

The opening cite tag starts its area and the matching closing tag ends it. Keep the opening tag before the affected words and the closing tag immediately after them.

Use this cite sample as a starting point, then change one piece at a time:

<figure>
  <blockquote>
    <p>
      It was a bright cold day in April, and the clocks were striking thirteen.
    </p>
  </blockquote>
  <figcaption>
    First sentence in
    <cite
      ><a href="http://www.george-orwell.org/1984/0.html"
        >Nineteen Eighty-Four</a
      ></cite
    >
    by George Orwell (Part 1, Chapter 1).
  </figcaption>
</figure>

How this sample works: Here cite supplies the meaning described above; the surrounding tags provide the context needed for the example to make sense.
