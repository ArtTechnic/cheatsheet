word-break

Changing word-break changes rules for word breaking. It changes the presentation or flow of writing.

Different values give word-break different instructions.

Important: This is an older feature. You may meet it in old websites, but you should normally choose a modern alternative for new work.

A practical word-break example is shown below:

word-break: normal;

What to notice: In this case word-break receives normal.
