ruby

In an HTML document, ruby represents ruby annotation. It works on a small piece of text inside a sentence, like placing a useful label or emphasis around selected words.

Treat the two ruby tags like the covers of a folder: the related content goes between them. Keep the opening tag before the affected words and the closing tag immediately after them.

A small ruby example looks like this:

<ruby> 明日 <rp>(</rp><rt>Ashita</rt><rp>)</rp> </ruby>

The important part: Find <ruby> in the sample first. Its attributes and enclosed content provide the information this element needs.
