ol

In an HTML document, ol represents ordered list. It is one of the building blocks used to organise related items into a list instead of leaving them as loose paragraphs.

Put the content belonging to ol between its opening and closing tags. Lists have a container and items inside it, so check that each item is nested inside the correct kind of list.

Important: This is an older feature. You may meet it in old websites, but you should normally choose a modern alternative for new work.

Try the following ol code in a spare practice file:

<ol>
  <li>Mix flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt.</li>
  <li>In another bowl, mix eggs, milk, and oil.</li>
  <li>Stir both mixtures together.</li>
  <li>Fill muffin tray 3/4 full.</li>
  <li>Bake for 20 minutes.</li>
</ol>

How this sample works: This sample puts ol into a small piece of realistic HTML. Change its content or one attribute at a time and watch what differs.
