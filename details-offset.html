offset

Think of offset as the control for shorthand for offset distances. It controls movement or a visual change over time or space.

Place a colon after offset, then supply the value you want. When testing this property, remember: use gentle motion and remember that some visitors ask their device to reduce animation.

Here is an offset example to try in a practice page:

offset: path("M 20 60 L 120 60 L 70 10 L 20 60") 0% auto 90deg;

The important part: Here, path("M 20 60 L 120 60 L 70 10 L 20 60") 0% auto 90deg is the value attached to offset. Change it, refresh the page, and compare the new result with the original.
