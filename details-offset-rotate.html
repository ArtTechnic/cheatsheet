offset-rotate

In CSS, offset-rotate is the setting for rotation for offset-positioned element. It controls movement or a visual change over time or space.

A rule using offset-rotate needs a value after the colon.

A practical offset-rotate example is shown below:

offset-rotate: auto;

The important part: In this case offset-rotate receives auto.
