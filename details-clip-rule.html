clip-rule

The clip-rule property controls fill rule for shapes. It helps decide whether a box is shown and what happens when its contents do not fit normally.

The value written after clip-rule: chooses the result. While experimenting, keep in mind that hidden or clipped content may become impossible to reach, so test with more content than you expect.

Here is a clip-rule example to try in a practice page:

/* Keywords */
clip-rule: nonzero;
clip-rule: evenodd;

/* Global values */
clip-rule: inherit;
clip-rule: initial;
clip-rule: revert;
clip-rule: revert-layer;
clip-rule: unset;

What this example does: The important instruction in the sample is nonzero. It is assigned to clip-rule and changes only the part described above.
