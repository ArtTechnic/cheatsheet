animation-name

Choose animation-name when the design needs different animation name. It controls movement or a visual change over time or space.

Add animation-name inside a CSS rule and follow it with a colon and value. A helpful beginner check is this: Use gentle motion and remember that some visitors ask their device to reduce animation.

This short sample shows animation-name in use:

animation-name: none;

The important part: The important instruction in the sample is none. It is assigned to animation-name and changes only the part described above.
