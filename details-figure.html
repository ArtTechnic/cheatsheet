figure

Browsers read figure as a label meaning figure with caption. This tag helps place visual or audio material on the page, or supplies information that belongs with that material.

Put the content belonging to figure between its opening and closing tags. Remember that visitors may not see or hear the media, so provide useful text alternatives or captions when the tag supports them.

Try the following figure code in a spare practice file:

<figure>
  <img
    src="https://mdn.github.iohttps://mdn.github.iohttps://mdn.github.io/shared-assets/images/examples/elephant.jpg"
    alt="Elephant at sunset" />
  <figcaption>An elephant at sunset</figcaption>
</figure>

How this sample works: Start with the figure tag in the code, then inspect what sits inside it and which attributes have been added to its opening tag.
