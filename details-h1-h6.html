h1, h2, h3, h4, h5, h6

In an HTML document, h1, h2, h3, h4, h5, h6 represents heading levels 1-6. This tag helps organise writing so readers and browsers can understand how the words fit together.

The opening h1, h2, h3, h4, h5, h6 tag starts its area and the matching closing tag ends it. Do not choose it just because of its default size or spacing; choose it when its meaning matches the writing.

A practical h1, h2, h3, h4, h5, h6 example is shown below:

<h1>Beetles</h1>
<h2>External morphology</h2>
<h3>Head</h3>
<h4>Mouthparts</h4>
<h3>Thorax</h3>
<h4>Prothorax</h4>
<h4>Pterothorax</h4>

What to notice: The sample demonstrates <h1>. Its attributes and enclosed content provide the information this element needs.
