sup

The name sup tells you what it represents: superscript. It works on a small piece of text inside a sentence, like placing a useful label or emphasis around selected words.

Anything meant to belong to sup normally sits inside the pair of tags. Keep the opening tag before the affected words and the closing tag immediately after them.

The code below shows a simple use of sup:

<p>
  The <em>Pythagorean theorem</em> is often expressed as the following equation:
</p>

<p>
  <var>a<sup>2</sup></var> + <var>b<sup>2</sup></var> = <var>c<sup>2</sup></var>
</p>

The important part: Start with the sup tag in the code, then inspect what sits inside it and which attributes have been added to its opening tag.
