caption

A caption is like a title for your table. Just like every book has a title on the front cover, every table should have a caption that tells people what the table is about. The caption goes inside the table element, right at the very top -- before anything else.

Writing a caption is important because it helps everyone understand the table faster. People who use screen readers (these are tools that read the page out loud for people who can't see well) will hear the caption first. This means they know what to expect before they start listening to all the numbers and words in the table. Always write one clear, short sentence that explains what the table shows.

If you ever want to move the caption to the bottom of the table instead of the top, you can do that with the caption-side CSS property -- but that is covered on a different page in this cheat sheet.



<table>
    <caption>Monthly total</caption>
    <thead>
        <tr>
            <th>Month</th>
            <th>Total</th>
        </tr>
    </thead>
    <tbody>
        <tr>
            <td>August</td>
            <td>12</td>
        </tr>
    </tbody>
</table>
