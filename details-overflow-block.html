overflow-block

In CSS, overflow-block is the setting for overflow in block dimension. It helps decide whether a box is shown and what happens when its contents do not fit normally.

Different values give overflow-block different instructions. Try them one at a time, and remember that hidden or clipped content may become impossible to reach, so test with more content than you expect.

Use this overflow-block sample as a starting point, then change one piece at a time:

/* Keyword values */
overflow-block: visible;
overflow-block: hidden;
overflow-block: clip;
overflow-block: scroll;
overflow-block: auto;

/* Global values */
overflow-block: inherit;
overflow-block: initial;
overflow-block: revert;
overflow-block: revert-layer;
overflow-block: unset;

The important part: In this case overflow-block receives visible. Trying a contrasting valid value makes the difference easier to spot.
