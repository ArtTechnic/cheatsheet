scroll-snap-type

The scroll-snap-type property controls scroll snapping. It helps decide whether a box is shown and what happens when its contents do not fit normally.

Place a colon after scroll-snap-type, then supply the value you want.

A practical scroll-snap-type example is shown below:

scroll-snap-type: none;

How this sample works: The sample asks the browser to use none for scroll-snap-type.
