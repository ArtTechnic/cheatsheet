animation-timing-function

The browser looks at animation-timing-function when working out timing function. It controls movement or a visual change over time or space.

CSS reads this as the name animation-timing-function, a colon, and then a value. As you compare values, use gentle motion and remember that some visitors ask their device to reduce animation.

Here is a compact animation-timing-function example you can inspect and alter:

animation-timing-function: linear;

What this example does: Here, linear is the value attached to animation-timing-function. Change it, refresh the page, and compare the new result with the original.
