shape-image-threshold

Different values give shape-image-threshold different instructions.

Use this shape-image-threshold sample as a starting point, then change one piece at a time:

shape-outside: linear-gradient(
  50deg,
  rgb(77 26 103),
  transparent 80%,
  transparent
);
shape-image-threshold: 0.2;

What this example does: The important instruction in the sample is linear-gradient( 50deg, rgb(77 26 103), transparent 80%, transparent ). It is assigned to shape-image-threshold and changes only the part described above.
