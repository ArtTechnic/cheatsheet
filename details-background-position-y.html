background-position-y

Changing background-position-y changes vertical position of background image. It changes how something looks rather than changing what the HTML means.

Different values give background-position-y different instructions. Try them one at a time, and remember that check that text remains easy to read and that important information does not depend on colour alone.

A practical background-position-y example is shown below:

background-position-y: top;

What to notice: Read the line as ‘set background-position-y to top.’ That value is the instruction controlling what you see.
