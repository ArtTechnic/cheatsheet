clip

Choose clip when the design needs different clipping region of absolutely positioned element. It helps decide whether a box is shown and what happens when its contents do not fit normally.

Place a colon after clip, then supply the value you want. When testing this property, remember: hidden or clipped content may become impossible to reach, so test with more content than you expect.

Important: This is an older feature. You may meet it in old websites, but you should normally choose a modern alternative for new work.

Here is a compact clip example you can inspect and alter:

/* Keyword value */
clip: auto;

/* <shape> values */
clip: rect(1px, 10em, 3rem, 2ch);

/* Global values */
clip: inherit;
clip: initial;
clip: revert;
clip: revert-layer;
clip: unset;

How this sample works: In this case clip receives auto. Trying a contrasting valid value makes the difference easier to spot.
