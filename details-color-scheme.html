color-scheme

Use color-scheme when you need to change supported color schemes of document. It changes how something looks rather than changing what the HTML means.

Place a colon after color-scheme, then supply the value you want.

Here is a compact color-scheme example you can inspect and alter:

color-scheme: normal;

Reading the example: Read the line as ‘set color-scheme to normal.’ That value is the instruction controlling what you see.
