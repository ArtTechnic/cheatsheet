list-style-image

In CSS, list-style-image is the setting for image for list items. It changes list markers, numbering, or generated punctuation.

Place a colon after list-style-image, then supply the value you want.

This short sample shows list-style-image in use:

list-style-image: url("https://mdn.github.iohttps://mdn.github.iohttps://mdn.github.io/shared-assets/images/examples/rocket.svg");

What to notice: Read the line as ‘set list-style-image to url("https://mdn.github.iohttps://mdn.github.iohttps://mdn.github.io/shared-assets/images/examples/rocket.svg").’ That value is the instruction controlling what you see.
