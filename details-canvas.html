canvas

The name canvas tells you what it represents: scriptable drawing area. This tag helps place visual or audio material on the page, or supplies information that belongs with that material.

The opening canvas tag starts its area and the matching closing tag ends it. Remember that visitors may not see or hear the media, so provide useful text alternatives or captions when the tag supports them.

Important: This is an older feature. You may meet it in old websites, but you should normally choose a modern alternative for new work.

Try the following canvas code in a spare practice file:

<canvas width="120" height="120">
  An alternative text describing what your canvas displays.
</canvas>

How this sample works: This sample puts canvas into a small piece of realistic HTML. Change its content or one attribute at a time and watch what differs.
