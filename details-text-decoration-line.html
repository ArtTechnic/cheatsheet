text-decoration-line

The text-decoration-line property controls line of text decoration. It changes the presentation or flow of writing.

Place a colon after text-decoration-line, then supply the value you want.

A practical text-decoration-line example is shown below:

text-decoration-line: none;

How this sample works: Here, none is the value attached to text-decoration-line.
