pre

In an HTML document, pre represents preformatted text. This tag helps organise writing so readers and browsers can understand how the words fit together.

The opening pre tag starts its area and the matching closing tag ends it. Do not choose it just because of its default size or spacing; choose it when its meaning matches the writing.

Here is a pre example to try in a practice page:

<pre>
             S
             A
            LUT
             M
            O N
            D  E
            DONT
          JE SUIS
          LA  LAN
          G U E  É
         L O Q U E N
        TE      QUESA
       B  O  U  C  H  E
      O        P A R I S
     T I R E   ET   TIRERA
    T O U             JOURS
   AUX                  A  L
 LEM                      ANDS   - Apollinaire
</pre>

The important part: The sample demonstrates <pre>. Its attributes and enclosed content provide the information this element needs.
