border-left-color

Choose border-left-color when the design needs different color of left border. It changes how something looks rather than changing what the HTML means.

Different values give border-left-color different instructions. Try them one at a time, and remember that check that text remains easy to read and that important information does not depend on colour alone.

Here is a compact border-left-color example you can inspect and alter:

border-left-color: red;

What this example does: Here, red is the value attached to border-left-color. Change it, refresh the page, and compare the new result with the original.
