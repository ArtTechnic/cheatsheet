border-left

The border-left property controls shorthand for left border. It changes a line, edge, or corner around an element.

The value written after border-left: chooses the result. While experimenting, keep in mind that borders take up space in the box; outlines usually draw outside it without changing the layout.

Here is a compact border-left example you can inspect and alter:

border-left: solid;

What this example does: In this case border-left receives solid. Trying a contrasting valid value makes the difference easier to spot.
