textarea

The name textarea tells you what it represents: multi-line text input. It is a form building block: forms let a visitor enter information, choose something, or start an action.

The opening textarea tag starts its area and the matching closing tag ends it. Every control needs a clear label, and the page should still make sense when somebody uses only a keyboard.

A practical textarea example is shown below:

<label for="story">Tell us your story:</label>

<textarea id="story" name="story" rows="5" cols="33">
It was a dark and stormy night...
</textarea>

What to notice: The key piece in this sample is <textarea>. Read outward from that tag to see what belongs to it and how it is configured.
