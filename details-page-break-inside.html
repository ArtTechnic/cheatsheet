page-break-inside

The browser looks at page-break-inside when working out legacy page break control. It controls how content is divided across columns, pages, or separate pieces.

Place a colon after page-break-inside, then supply the value you want.

Important: This is an older feature. You may meet it in old websites, but you should normally choose a modern alternative for new work.

A practical page-break-inside example is shown below:

page-break-inside: auto;

What this example does: Read the line as ‘set page-break-inside to auto.’ That value is the instruction controlling what you see.
