font-size

Choose font-size when the design needs different font size. It helps decide how large a box or part of a box becomes.

The value written after font-size: chooses the result. While experimenting, keep in mind that padding and borders can also affect the final visible size, especially when box-sizing changes.

Use this font-size sample as a starting point, then change one piece at a time:

font-size: 1.2rem;

Reading the example: The important instruction in the sample is 1.2rem. It is assigned to font-size and changes only the part described above.
