overscroll-behavior

The overscroll-behavior property controls behavior when scrolling hits boundary. It helps decide whether a box is shown and what happens when its contents do not fit normally.

Add overscroll-behavior inside a CSS rule and follow it with a colon and value.

This short sample shows overscroll-behavior in use:

overscroll-behavior: auto;

The important part: The sample asks the browser to use auto for overscroll-behavior.
