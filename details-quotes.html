quotes

The quotes property controls quotation marks for q/blockquote. It changes list markers, numbering, or generated punctuation.

Add quotes inside a CSS rule and follow it with a colon and value.

Use this quotes sample as a starting point, then change one piece at a time:

quotes: none;

The important part: In this case quotes receives none.
