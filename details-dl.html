dl

The name dl tells you what it represents: description list. It is one of the building blocks used to organise related items into a list instead of leaving them as loose paragraphs.

Treat the two dl tags like the covers of a folder: the related content goes between them. Lists have a container and items inside it, so check that each item is nested inside the correct kind of list.

Important: This is an older feature. You may meet it in old websites, but you should normally choose a modern alternative for new work.

This short sample shows dl in use:

<p>Cryptids of Cornwall:</p>

<dl>
  <dt>Beast of Bodmin</dt>
  <dd>A large feline inhabiting Bodmin Moor.</dd>

  <dt>Morgawr</dt>
  <dd>A sea serpent.</dd>

  <dt>Owlman</dt>
  <dd>A giant owl-like creature.</dd>
</dl>

The important part: The sample demonstrates <dl>.
