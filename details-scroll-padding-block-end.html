scroll-padding-block-end

It changes space around, inside, or between boxes.

Place a colon after scroll-padding-block-end, then supply the value you want.

This short sample shows scroll-padding-block-end in use:

scroll-padding-block-end: 0;

Reading the example: Here, 0 is the value attached to scroll-padding-block-end.
