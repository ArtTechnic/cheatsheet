label

Browsers read label as a label meaning label for form control. It is a form building block: forms let a visitor enter information, choose something, or start an action.

The opening label tag starts its area and the matching closing tag ends it. Every control needs a clear label, and the page should still make sense when somebody uses only a keyboard.

This short sample shows label in use:

<div class="preference">
  <label for="cheese">I like cheese.</label>
  <input type="checkbox" name="cheese" id="cheese" />
</div>

<div class="preference">
  <label for="peas">I like peas.</label>
  <input type="checkbox" name="peas" id="peas" />
</div>

What this example does: This sample puts label into a small piece of realistic HTML. Change its content or one attribute at a time and watch what differs.
