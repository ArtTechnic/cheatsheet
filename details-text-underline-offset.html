text-underline-offset

With text-underline-offset, you can control offset of text underline. It controls movement or a visual change over time or space.

Add text-underline-offset inside a CSS rule and follow it with a colon and value. A helpful beginner check is this: Use gentle motion and remember that some visitors ask their device to reduce animation.

Here is a text-underline-offset example to try in a practice page:

text-underline-offset: auto;

What to notice: The important instruction in the sample is auto. It is assigned to text-underline-offset and changes only the part described above.
