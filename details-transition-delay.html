transition-delay

In CSS, transition-delay is the setting for delay before transition starts. It controls movement or a visual change over time or space.

Add transition-delay inside a CSS rule and follow it with a colon and value. A helpful beginner check is this: Use gentle motion and remember that some visitors ask their device to reduce animation.

A practical transition-delay example is shown below:

transition-delay: 250ms;
transition-property: margin-right;

The important part: The sample asks the browser to use 250ms for transition-delay. The property stays the same when you experiment; the value is the part to change.
