map

Use map when a piece of HTML represents image map. This tag helps place visual or audio material on the page, or supplies information that belongs with that material.

Anything meant to belong to map normally sits inside the pair of tags. Remember that visitors may not see or hear the media, so provide useful text alternatives or captions when the tag supports them.

Here is a compact map example you can inspect and alter:

<map name="infographic">
  <area
    shape="poly"
    coords="130,147,200,107,254,219,130,228"
    href="https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/HTML"
    alt="HTML" />
  <area
    shape="poly"
    coords="130,147,130,228,6,219,59,107"
    href="https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/CSS"
    alt="CSS" />
  <area
    shape="poly"
    coords="130,147,200,107,130,4,59,107"
    href="https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/JavaScript"
    alt="JavaScript" />
</map>
