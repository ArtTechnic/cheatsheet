mix-blend-mode

The browser looks at mix-blend-mode when working out blend mode. It changes how something looks rather than changing what the HTML means.

Add mix-blend-mode inside a CSS rule and follow it with a colon and value. A helpful beginner check is this: Check that text remains easy to read and that important information does not depend on colour alone.

Here is a compact mix-blend-mode example you can inspect and alter:

mix-blend-mode: normal;

The important part: Here, normal is the value attached to mix-blend-mode. Change it, refresh the page, and compare the new result with the original.
