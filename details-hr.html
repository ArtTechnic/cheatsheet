hr

Browsers read hr as a label meaning thematic break. This tag helps organise writing so readers and browsers can understand how the words fit together.

Because hr cannot wrap text or other elements, it appears as one void tag. Do not choose it just because of its default size or spacing; choose it when its meaning matches the writing.

Important: This is an older feature. You may meet it in old websites, but you should normally choose a modern alternative for new work.

A small hr example looks like this:

<p>§1: The first rule of Fight Club is: You do not talk about Fight Club.</p>

<hr />

<p>§2: The second rule of Fight Club is: Always bring cupcakes.</p>

What to notice: Find <hr> in the sample first. Its attributes and enclosed content provide the information this element needs.
