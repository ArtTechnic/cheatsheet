data

When you write data, you are marking something as machine-readable value. It works on a small piece of text inside a sentence, like placing a useful label or emphasis around selected words.

Treat the two data tags like the covers of a folder: the related content goes between them. Keep the opening tag before the affected words and the closing tag immediately after them.

The code below shows a simple use of data:

<p>New Products:</p>
<ul>
  <li><data value="398">Mini Ketchup</data></li>
  <li><data value="399">Jumbo Ketchup</data></li>
  <li><data value="400">Mega Jumbo Ketchup</data></li>
</ul>

What to notice: The key piece in this sample is <data>. Read outward from that tag to see what belongs to it and how it is configured.
