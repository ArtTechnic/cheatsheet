border-image

The border-image property controls shorthand for border image. It changes a line, edge, or corner around an element.

The value written after border-image: chooses the result.

This short sample shows border-image in use:

border-image: url("https://mdn.github.iohttps://mdn.github.iohttps://mdn.github.io/shared-assets/images/examples/border-diamonds.png") 30;

How this sample works: Here, url("https://mdn.github.iohttps://mdn.github.iohttps://mdn.github.io/shared-assets/images/examples/border-diamonds.png") 30 is the value attached to border-image.
