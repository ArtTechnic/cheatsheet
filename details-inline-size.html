inline-size

Choose inline-size when the design needs different logical width of inline element. It helps decide how large a box or part of a box becomes.

Place a colon after inline-size, then supply the value you want. When testing this property, remember: padding and borders can also affect the final visible size, especially when box-sizing changes.

Use this inline-size sample as a starting point, then change one piece at a time:

inline-size: 150px;
writing-mode: horizontal-tb;

Reading the example: Here, 150px is the value attached to inline-size. Change it, refresh the page, and compare the new result with the original.
