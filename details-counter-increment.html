counter-increment

In CSS, counter-increment is the setting for increment counters. It changes list markers, numbering, or generated punctuation.

Place a colon after counter-increment, then supply the value you want. When testing this property, remember: keep the result understandable even when custom decoration is unavailable.

This short sample shows counter-increment in use:

counter-increment: example-counter;

What to notice: This declaration gives counter-increment the value example-counter. Replace that value with another valid choice to compare the two results.
