audio

Browsers read audio as a label meaning audio media. This tag helps place visual or audio material on the page, or supplies information that belongs with that material.

Put the content belonging to audio between its opening and closing tags. Remember that visitors may not see or hear the media, so provide useful text alternatives or captions when the tag supports them.

Important: This feature is experimental. Check browser support before relying on it.

Here is a compact audio example you can inspect and alter:

<figure>
  <figcaption>Listen to the T-Rex:</figcaption>
  <audio controls src="https://mdn.github.iohttps://mdn.github.iohttps://mdn.github.io/shared-assets/audio/t-rex-roar.mp3"></audio>
  <a href="https://mdn.github.iohttps://mdn.github.iohttps://mdn.github.io/shared-assets/audio/t-rex-roar.mp3"> Download audio </a>
</figure>

What to notice: The key piece in this sample is <audio>. Read outward from that tag to see what belongs to it and how it is configured.
