overflow-wrap

Use overflow-wrap when you need to change breaks long words. It helps decide whether a box is shown and what happens when its contents do not fit normally.

A rule using overflow-wrap needs a value after the colon.

A practical overflow-wrap example is shown below:

overflow-wrap: normal;

The important part: Read the line as ‘set overflow-wrap to normal.’ That value is the instruction controlling what you see.
