footer

In an HTML document, footer represents footer content. It identifies what kind of section this is, rather like putting a label on a folder before placing papers inside it.

Anything meant to belong to footer normally sits inside the pair of tags. Choose it because the content has that meaning. If you only need an unlabelled box for styling, a div may be the clearer choice.

A small footer example looks like this:

<article>
  <h1>How to be a wizard</h1>
  <ol>
    <li>Grow a long, majestic beard.</li>
    <li>Wear a tall, pointed hat.</li>
    <li>Have I mentioned the beard?</li>
  </ol>
  <footer>
    <p>© 2018 Gandalf</p>
  </footer>
</article>

What to notice: The key piece in this sample is <footer>. Read outward from that tag to see what belongs to it and how it is configured.
