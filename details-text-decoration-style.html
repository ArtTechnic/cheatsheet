text-decoration-style

Choose text-decoration-style when the design needs different style of text decoration. It changes the presentation or flow of writing.

The value written after text-decoration-style: chooses the result. While experimenting, keep in mind that test several lines, long words, and zoomed text—a single short sentence can hide problems.

Use this text-decoration-style sample as a starting point, then change one piece at a time:

text-decoration-style: solid;

What to notice: The sample asks the browser to use solid for text-decoration-style. The property stays the same when you experiment; the value is the part to change.
