display

Changing display changes display type. It helps decide whether a box is shown and what happens when its contents do not fit normally.

The value written after display: chooses the result. While experimenting, keep in mind that hidden or clipped content may become impossible to reach, so test with more content than you expect.

The code below shows a simple use of display:

display: block;

What to notice: Here, block is the value attached to display. Change it, refresh the page, and compare the new result with the original.
