param

When you write param, you are marking something as parameter for object. It places another resource or browsing area inside the current page, like making a window that looks into something else.

Unlike most tags, param is a void element: it has no closing tag and holds no content between tags. Embedded material can affect security, speed, and accessibility, so include only resources you trust and actually need.

Important: This is an older feature. You may meet it in old websites, but you should normally choose a modern alternative for new work.

Use this param sample as a starting point, then change one piece at a time:

<param name="..." value="...">

Reading the example: The key piece in this sample is <param>. Read outward from that tag to see what belongs to it and how it is configured.
