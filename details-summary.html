summary

In an HTML document, summary represents summary for details. It creates a part of the page that a visitor can open, close, or interact with instead of only reading it.

The opening summary tag starts its area and the matching closing tag ends it. Make its purpose clear in the visible text so the visitor knows what will happen before using it.

A small summary example looks like this:

<details>
  <summary>
    I have keys but no doors. I have space but no room. You can enter but can't
    leave. What am I?
  </summary>
  A keyboard.
</details>

How this sample works: This sample puts summary into a small piece of realistic HTML. Change its content or one attribute at a time and watch what differs.
