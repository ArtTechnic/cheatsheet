line-break

With line-break, you can control line breaking rules. It changes the presentation or flow of writing.

The value written after line-break: chooses the result. While experimenting, keep in mind that test several lines, long words, and zoomed text—a single short sentence can hide problems.

A small line-break example looks like this:

line-break: auto;

Reading the example: The colon separates line-break from its chosen value, auto. The semicolon marks the end of this declaration.
