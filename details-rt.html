rt

When you write rt, you are marking something as ruby text. It works on a small piece of text inside a sentence, like placing a useful label or emphasis around selected words.

The opening rt tag starts its area and the matching closing tag ends it. Keep the opening tag before the affected words and the closing tag immediately after them.

Use this rt sample as a starting point, then change one piece at a time:

<ruby>
  漢 <rp>(</rp><rt>kan</rt><rp>)</rp> 字 <rp>(</rp><rt>ji</rt><rp>)</rp>
</ruby>

What this example does: Find <rt> in the sample first. Its attributes and enclosed content provide the information this element needs.
