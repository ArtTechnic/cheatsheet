offset-distance

With offset-distance, you can control distance of offset position. It controls movement or a visual change over time or space.

The value written after offset-distance: chooses the result.

Use this offset-distance sample as a starting point, then change one piece at a time:

offset-distance: 0%;

How this sample works: Read the line as ‘set offset-distance to 0%.’ That value is the instruction controlling what you see.
