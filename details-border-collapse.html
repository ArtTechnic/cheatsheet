border-collapse

A border-collapse setting of collapse makes the lines around your table cells merge into a single line. Normally each cell has its own border, so where two cells touch there are two lines side by side. With collapse, those two lines become one. This makes the table look cleaner and more like a real grid.

Use collapse when you want your table to look neat and grid-like. If you prefer a bit of space between the cells, use the separate value instead. The separate value is the default, so if you do nothing, cells stay separate.

table {
    border-collapse: collapse;
}
