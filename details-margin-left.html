margin-left

The margin-left property controls left margin. It changes space around, inside, or between boxes.

Place a colon after margin-left, then supply the value you want.

This short sample shows margin-left in use:

margin-left: 1em;

Reading the example: Read the line as ‘set margin-left to 1em.’ That value is the instruction controlling what you see.
