transition-property

In CSS, transition-property is the setting for property for transition. It controls movement or a visual change over time or space.

CSS reads this as the name transition-property, a colon, and then a value. As you compare values, use gentle motion and remember that some visitors ask their device to reduce animation.

A practical transition-property example is shown below:

transition-property: margin-right;

What to notice: This declaration gives transition-property the value margin-right. Replace that value with another valid choice to compare the two results.
