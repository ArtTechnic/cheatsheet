background-color

Choose background-color when the design needs different background color of an element. It changes how something looks rather than changing what the HTML means.

Place a colon after background-color, then supply the value you want. When testing this property, remember: check that text remains easy to read and that important information does not depend on colour alone.

Use this background-color sample as a starting point, then change one piece at a time:

background-color: brown;

What to notice: In this case background-color receives brown. Trying a contrasting valid value makes the difference easier to spot.
