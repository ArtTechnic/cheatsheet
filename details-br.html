br

Use br when a piece of HTML represents line break. This tag helps organise writing so readers and browsers can understand how the words fit together.

Unlike most tags, br is a void element: it has no closing tag and holds no content between tags. Do not choose it just because of its default size or spacing; choose it when its meaning matches the writing.

Important: This is an older feature. You may meet it in old websites, but you should normally choose a modern alternative for new work.

Here is a compact br example you can inspect and alter:

<p>
  O'er all the hilltops<br />
  Is quiet now,<br />
  In all the treetops<br />
  Hearest thou<br />
  Hardly a breath;<br />
  The birds are asleep in the trees:<br />
  Wait, soon like these<br />
  Thou too shalt rest.
</p>

How this sample works: The key piece in this sample is <br>. Read outward from that tag to see what belongs to it and how it is configured.
