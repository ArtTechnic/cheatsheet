track

Browsers read track as a label meaning text tracks for media. This tag helps place visual or audio material on the page, or supplies information that belongs with that material.

Because track cannot wrap text or other elements, it appears as one void tag. Remember that visitors may not see or hear the media, so provide useful text alternatives or captions when the tag supports them.

This short sample shows track in use:

What this example does: Here track supplies the meaning described above; the surrounding tags provide the context needed for the example to make sense.
