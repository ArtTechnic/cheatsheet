ins

Use ins when a piece of HTML represents inserted text. Think of it as an HTML building block specifically meant for inserted text.

Put the content belonging to ins between its opening and closing tags. Use it when that description matches the content, not simply because its default appearance seems useful.

Use this ins sample as a starting point, then change one piece at a time:

<p>&ldquo;You're late!&rdquo;</p>
<del>
  <p>&ldquo;I apologize for the delay.&rdquo;</p>
</del>
<ins cite="../how-to-be-a-wizard.html" datetime="2018-05">
  <p>&ldquo;A wizard is never late &hellip;&rdquo;</p>
</ins>

What to notice: This sample puts ins into a small piece of realistic HTML. Change its content or one attribute at a time and watch what differs.
