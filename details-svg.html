svg

The svg tag marks part of the page as scalable Vector Graphics container. This tag helps place visual or audio material on the page, or supplies information that belongs with that material.

Put the content belonging to svg between its opening and closing tags. Remember that visitors may not see or hear the media, so provide useful text alternatives or captions when the tag supports them.

A practical svg example is shown below:

<svg viewBox="0 0 100 100" width="100" height="100">
    <circle cx="50" cy="50" r="40" fill="crimson"></circle>
</svg>

Reading the example: The key piece in this sample is <svg>. Read outward from that tag to see what belongs to it and how it is configured.
